# Search gold codebook

You are grading which saved pieces answer a question someone asked their own
knowledge base by voice. Grade each candidate piece against the question.

- **Scope.** Only what was delivered to them or saved by them counts. If nothing
  matches, the answer is "nothing".
- **Subject, not words.** A piece counts by its subject, not by shared words.
- **Multi-part questions.** A piece answering one part is correct. "Ideally"
  marks a preference, not a requirement.
- **Time phrases** ("last session", "in April") restrict candidates by date.
- **Their own words.** When the question asks what they said, decided or
  considered, judge by what they said back in the session.
- **Target questions** describe one piece they remember. What identifies it is
  anything they say they remember about it: usually a specific example, claim
  or idea, sometimes its medium, speaker or rough date. A detail counts against
  a piece only when the piece contradicts it; a detail the piece does not
  mention is not a mismatch. When the question asks for that piece's own
  content ("its arguments", "other takeaways from it"), another piece on the
  same idea does not answer it. Whether a piece is the one they meant
  (`primary`) is their call, never the grader's.

## Grades

- **2, relevant:** answers the question or one part of it.
- **1, partial:** one relevant line inside a piece about something else.
- **0, not relevant:** same subject without answering it, or another subject.
  Grade-0 pieces are not listed in the gold.

**Counter** (grade 2 only): the piece argues against the question's premise
with its own reasoning. A piece outside the question's scope is grade 0, not
counter.

## One need, several uris

A need is one piece of information. When the same piece is stored twice (the
same corpus item under two urls), both uris go in one need. A session is listed
when it is where the piece was delivered.
